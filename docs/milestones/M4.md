## Important Decisions

### Unknown Economics Is Not Zero

Missing provider evidence remains unknown rather than being fabricated as zero.

### Native Cost vs Estimated Cost

Where a provider exposes native cost, it can be preferred. Otherwise a
versioned pricing catalog may estimate cost.

### Privacy by Default

Default telemetry must not casually persist full prompts, source content,
secret-like values, or unsafe exception payloads.
